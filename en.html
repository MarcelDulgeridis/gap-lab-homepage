Short answer: what is THE GAP Lab?

THE GAP Lab designs, develops, delivers and markets people development programmes in three focus areas: Strategy, Sales & Innovation, Leadership & Organisation and Finance & Transformation. Specific offerings are five on-site InnoLab workshops and the free AI Readiness Check.

Frequently asked questions

Answers in brief.

What does THE GAP Lab do?

It designs, develops, delivers and markets people development programmes, such as sales and negotiation training, leadership development and finance for non-finance managers. Focus areas: Strategy, Sales & Innovation, Leadership & Organisation and Finance & Transformation. It is based in Altdorf bei Nürnberg.

Who is THE GAP Lab for?

For senior management, HR managers and leaders who plan or procure development in a targeted way, as well as for chambers, training providers and academies.

Which focus area suits which task?

Strategy, Sales & Innovation when offering, sales, negotiation or change need direction; Leadership & Organisation when roles and decision paths are unclear; Finance & Transformation when figures need to support decisions.

What happens in the free initial consultation?

In 20 to 30 minutes via Microsoft Teams, we clarify the reason, objective and target group. You then receive a proposal with a clearly defined scope. To book: Contact.

What does working together cost?

After the initial consultation, you receive an individual proposal based on scope. Prices for the InnoLab formats are listed on the InnoLab page.
