Marketing and sales management, strategic management, and innovation and change management, complemented by negotiation, rhetoric, facilitation and conflict management. The aim: strategy, offering and presence mesh so that they land with customers and within the team.

Strategic management
Setting goals, priorities and resources and steering the direction of a company or business unit.
Sales management
Planning and managing sales: targets, target customers, processes and sales conversations.
Interests and positions
The position is the demand; the interest is the reason behind it. Knowing the interests opens up more solutions.
Alternative to a negotiated agreement (BATNA)
The best alternative if no agreement is reached (Best Alternative to a Negotiated Agreement). It determines up to which point an offer is acceptable.
Bargaining zone (ZOPA)
The range within which both sides could agree (Zone of Possible Agreement). Without overlap, agreement is unlikely.
Anchor
The first specific figure named in a discussion. It often shapes how the discussion proceeds.
Structuring arguments
Claim, reasoning and evidence, structured so that listeners can follow the argument.
Change management
Planning and supporting change: mindset, communication and dealing with resistance.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about this focus area.

What does the Strategy, Sales & Innovation focus area cover?

Marketing and sales management, strategic management, negotiation, rhetoric, facilitation, conflict management, team building, and innovation and change management, for example as an InnoLab for new product development or product enhancement.

Do you offer sales training?

Yes. Sales training and sales management are part of this focus area: conversation skills, negotiation, proposals and sales steering. We set out the scope in our proposal after the initial consultation.

What is the difference between negotiation and rhetoric?

Negotiation concerns strategy and preparation: goals, alternatives, room for manoeuvre and the course of the conversation. Rhetoric concerns impact: structuring arguments, choice of words and presence. The two complement each other.

Can a programme work with our own cases?

Yes. Your own negotiation, sales or innovation cases can be used in exercises, anonymised if required.

Who is the contact person?

Dr. Oliver Meschkat, Partner at The Gap Lab GmbH, specialising in marketing and sales management, people development, and innovation and change management. Appointments: Contact.
