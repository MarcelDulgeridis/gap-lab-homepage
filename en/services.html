Short answer: What does THE GAP Lab offer?

People development programmes: conceived, developed, delivered and marketed, in the focus areas Strategy, Sales & Innovation, Leadership & Organisation, and Finance & Transformation.

The four stages

From key question to outcome.

  1. 01 · StageConcept
    Key question

    What should the programme achieve, and for whom?

    What we do

    Clarify the reason, objective and target group, review programmes, derive the concept.

    Outcomes
    • Target state and target group
    • Concept paper with learning objectives and structure
    • Format and schedule
    • Success criteria
  2. 02 · StageDevelopment
    Key question

    How does the concept become practice for participants?

    What we do

    Tailor content, exercises and case studies to the participants' working practice.

    Outcomes
    • Materials for the workshop or training
    • Exercises and case studies
    • Guides and checklists for transfer into practice
    • Session plan
  3. 03 · StageDelivery
    Key question

    In which format is the programme most effective?

    What we do

    Workshop, training or ongoing support, delivered by the partners.

    Outcomes
    • Delivered programme
    • Materials for participants
    • Brief evaluation with recommendations
  4. 04 · StageMarketing
    Key question

    How are the benefits and the target group made clear?

    What we do

    Describe and position the programme as a clear offering.

    Outcomes
    • Description of the offering
    • Target group messaging
    • Information materials
