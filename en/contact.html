Frequently asked questions

Questions about the initial consultation.

Is there a charge for the initial consultation?

No. The initial consultation takes 20 to 30 minutes, is held via Microsoft Teams and is free of charge and without obligation.

How do I book an appointment?

Via the partners' calendars: choose a time and receive an Outlook invitation with a Teams link. Booking takes place on the parent company's website.

What should I prepare?

Four key points are enough: reason and objective, target group and approximate size, timeframe for impact, and previous experience.
