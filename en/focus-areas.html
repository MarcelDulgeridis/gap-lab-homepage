Frequently asked questions

Questions about the focus areas.

Which focus area suits which task?

Strategy, Sales & Innovation when your offering, sales, negotiation or change need direction; Leadership & Organisation when roles and decision paths are unclear and change has stalled; Finance & Transformation when figures need to support decisions. The initial consultation determines the right fit.

Who is the contact person for each focus area?

Dr. Oliver Meschkat for Strategy, Sales & Innovation, Prof. Dr. Constantin Schubart for Leadership & Organisation, Prof. Dr. Marcel Dulgeridis for Finance & Transformation.

Can focus areas be combined?

Yes. Programmes can be designed across focus areas. The free initial consultation clarifies which combination suits your situation.
