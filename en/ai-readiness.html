How it works

Eight signals. Two perspectives. One clear first step.

    Frequently asked questions

    Short answers

    Who is the check for?

    For senior management, partners, transformation offices, HR and IT in law and tax firms, professional services, financial services providers and mid-sized companies: wherever AI is meant to be more than a tool trial.

    What happens to my answers?

    Your answers stay in your browser. Only if you unlock the full report do we store your email address, organisation and result, so that we can offer you an interpretation. No newsletter, and you can withdraw your consent at any time. If you invite a colleague to compare, the link contains only your eight answer values, no name and no email address. For the team snapshot, only the eight answer values are stored, without name or email, in the EU. Only the average is visible, from 5 responses onwards; after 90 days everything is deleted automatically.

    How reliable is the result?

    It is an honest self-assessment from one perspective and therefore an indicative signal, not a certification. How big the gap really is only becomes clear with a real survey of leaders and employees in the full THE GAP Transformation Intelligence System.

    What does the check cost?

    Nothing. The check and the full report are free of charge. We only discuss further services if you wish.
