Glossary: the key terms explained clearly.

From strategy and negotiation to leadership and finance: these terms underpin THE GAP Lab's focus areas.

In brief

The glossary explains 22 terms from Strategy, Sales & Innovation (for example BATNA, ZOPA, change management), Leadership & Organisation (for example role clarity, implementation gap) and Finance & Transformation (for example cash flow, business case), as well as key THE GAP Lab terms. Alternative to a negotiated agreement (BATNA)Strategy, Sales & Innovation
The best alternative if no agreement is reached (Best Alternative to a Negotiated Agreement). It determines up to what point an offer is acceptable. Learn more
AnchorStrategy, Sales & Innovation
The first concrete figure in a conversation. It often shapes how the rest of the discussion unfolds. Learn more
Argument structureStrategy, Sales & Innovation
Claim, reasoning and evidence, so that listeners can follow the argument. Learn more
Balance sheetFinance & Transformation
Assets and capital (equity and liabilities) as at a reporting date. Learn more
Business caseFinance & Transformation
The economic justification for a project: costs, benefits, assumptions, risks. Learn more
Cash flowFinance & Transformation
Inflows and outflows of cash. Profit and liquidity can diverge. Learn more
Change managementStrategy, Sales & Innovation
Planning and supporting change: mindset, communication and dealing with resistance. Learn more
Decision rightsLeadership & Organisation
Who decides, who is involved, who is only informed. Learn more

Leadership conversationLeadership & Organisation
A structured conversation about goals, expectations and development. Learn more
Leadership cultureLeadership & Organisation
The norms lived out in everyday work: what is rewarded, tolerated, discussed? Learn more
Income statement (P&L)Finance & Transformation

InnoLabTHE GAP Lab

Interests and positionsStrategy, Sales & Innovation
The position is the demand; the interest is the reason behind it. Knowing the interests opens up more solutions. Learn more
Key figuresFinance & Transformation
Condensed metrics such as margin or equity ratio. They need context. Learn more

Role clarityLeadership & Organisation
