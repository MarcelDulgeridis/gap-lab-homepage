01

Innovation week

InnoLab Sprint

Ideas do not emerge in meetings. They emerge from prototypes.

5 days on site8–12 participants

Five days in which your teams take a real question all the way to a presentation-ready prototype.

  1. Day 1 · ChallengeCreative destructionSharpen the question, agree goals using OKR, first build with LEGO® Serious Play®.
  2. Day 2 · UnderstandCustomer and marketPersona, buying motives, PESTEL, Five Forces, Point of View.
  3. Day 3 · IdeasFrom first idea to modelBrainwriting, morphological box, Walt Disney method, 3D prototype.
  4. Day 4 · ImplementBusiness modelBusiness Model Canvas, benefits pyramid, key messages, Kanban and Scrum.
  5. Day 5 · PitchDragons' DenFive-minute pitch to decision-makers, joint assessment, transfer plan.

Benefits

  • Prototypes, not slides.
  • Future skills under real conditions.
  • Teams across boundaries.
Who it is for
Junior staff and managers, trainees, apprentices, interdisciplinary project teams
Outcome
Prototype · Business Model Canvas · pitch · transfer plan with named owners
Prerequisites
A flexible room, a real question, decision-makers on pitch day

02

Learning space consulting

InnoLab Design

A room is not a backdrop. It is a learning tool.

3 days on siteProject team

We analyse your training, meeting or project rooms and work with the people who use them to develop a concept for an Innovation Lab, prioritised by impact and budget.

  1. Day 1 · Current-state analysisMaking maturity visibleWalk-through, assessment of all design parameters, short survey of users.
  2. Day 2 · Co-designTurning those affected into those involvedWorkshop with all user groups, target vision for each parameter, first drafts.
  3. Day 3 · RoadmapImpact before effortCost-benefit assessment, prioritisation using the Eisenhower matrix, quick wins and expansion stages.
  4. OutcomeDecision paperMaturity profile, action plan and budget framework, for senior management or a funding application.

Benefits

  • Investment you can justify.
  • Acceptance, not resistance.
  • A room that grows with you.
Who it is for
Training centres, chambers, academies, HR and L&D departments, companies with project spaces
Outcome
Maturity profile · target vision · prioritised roadmap · budget framework
Optional
Implementation support and evaluation after the first quarter of operation

03

Train the Trainer

Learning Design 4.0

Knowledge is not transferred. It is constructed.

3 days on site6–12 participants

For vocational trainers, in-house trainers and lecturers who want to move their learning formats on from lecture-style teaching to active, playful and competence-based learning.

  1. Module 1Constructive AlignmentDesign learning objectives, methods and assessment as one coherent whole.
  2. Module 2DramaturgyA narrative arc across the days, inputs of no more than 20 to 30 minutes.
  3. Module 3Game-Based LearningTwelve success factors for games with a learning impact, including LEGO® Serious Play®.
  4. Module 4Methods toolkitChoose purposefully from around 40 proven creativity, analysis and reflection methods.
  5. Module 5Learning as equalsFacilitation, self-determined breaks, error culture and emotional intelligence in the seminar.
  6. TransferTry out and evaluateRevise your own format, test it with the group, evaluate it with feedback.

Benefits

  • Activation, not lecturing.
  • Ready to use immediately.
  • Impact you can demonstrate.
Who it is for
AEVO-qualified vocational trainers, in-house trainers, lecturers at academies and chambers, L&D teams
Outcome
Revised learning format · methods toolkit · evaluation form · transfer plan
Add-on
Observation and feedback during the first run of the new format
Package price

€8,300 net · approx. €692 per person with 12 participants

First step: format check of one seminar (free of charge)

04

Leadership training

Change Leadership

Change rarely fails because of the concept. It fails because of what it demands of people.

2 + 1 days on site8–14 participants

For managers and change agents who do not just decide on innovations but make them work in their teams.

  1. Starting pointInnovation typesActive, passive, reactive or random: where does your organisation stand?
  2. DiagnosisUnderstanding resistanceNot knowing and not wanting; factual, power-related and personal reasons.
  3. MindsetEmotional intelligenceRecognise feelings, convey purpose, create psychological safety.
  4. CommunicationChange communicationStorytelling, key messages, making quick wins visible.
  5. After 6 weeksTransfer dayReflect on your own project, adjust course, embed an error culture.

Benefits

  • Involved, not just affected.
  • Clarity, not information overload.
  • Implementation, not intention.
Who it is for
Managers at all levels, project leads, those responsible for innovation and change, multipliers
Outcome
Stakeholder and resistance analysis · communication plan · personal change roadmap
Methods
Case work, LEGO® Serious Play®, role play, peer consulting
Package price

€8,300 net · approx. €692 per person with 12 participants

Strategy workshop

Innovation Scorecard

Innovation does not need faith. It needs metrics.

2 days on site4–10 participants

For senior management and those responsible for innovation, HR and L&D who want to anchor Innovation Labs, learning formats or innovation programmes strategically and demonstrate their success.

  1. Day 1 · MorningAssessing where you standInnovation type, existing activities, check against 13 empirically derived success factors.
  2. Day 1 · AfternoonStrategy and mandateVision, mission, target groups, and what happens to the outcomes: core business, incubator or stop.
  3. Day 2 · MorningMetricsOngoing process assessment; usage, learning and market metrics; evaluation at micro and macro level.
  4. Day 2 · AfternoonBusiness caseCost-benefit analysis, maturity roadmap, decision paper and funding logic.

Benefits

  • Backing, not justification.
  • Resources that last.

Senior management, innovation, HR and L&D leads, controlling
