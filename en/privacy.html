Privacy policy

This English version is provided for convenience. Only the German version is legally binding.

Last updated: 10 November 2026

1. Controller

The Gap Lab GmbH, Hessenstraße 28, 90518 Altdorf bei Nürnberg, represented by the Managing Director Prof. Dr. Constantin Schubart.
Email: info@schubart-dulgeridis.de

2. Overview

This website does not use cookies or any analytics, tracking or marketing services. Fonts and images are served by gap-lab.de itself. One exception is the AI Readiness Check: it stores your progress locally in your browser and only connects to a database service if you unlock an evaluation or use a team profile (section 6).

3. Hosting and server log files

The website is hosted by Vercel Inc. (USA). When the website is accessed, the hosting provider processes technically necessary access data: IP address, date and time, page accessed, referrer, browser type and status code. The purpose is to deliver the website and ensure its stability and security. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a secure, functioning web presence). The data are stored only for as long as is necessary for this purpose. For the transfer of data to the USA, we rely on the safeguards provided by the provider pursuant to Art. 44 et seq. GDPR.

4. Contacting us

If you contact us by email or telephone, we process your details in order to handle your enquiry. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (pre-contractual measures) or Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (handling of enquiries). We delete the data as soon as the enquiry has been dealt with and no statutory retention obligations prevent deletion. Clicking an email link merely opens your local email program; no data are transmitted to us in the process.

5. Appointment booking

Initial consultations are booked on the website of our parent company, Schubart & Dulgeridis GmbH. When you click “Choose a time”, you leave gap-lab.de. Processing there is governed by its privacy policy.

6. AI Readiness Check

On the AI Readiness Check page, you respond to eight statements. Your browser calculates the result itself; your answers do not leave your device.

Local storage. So that you can pause and resume, the check stores your progress in your browser's local storage (localStorage) and session storage (sessionStorage), for example answers, result and, where applicable, campaign parameters from the link you used. These data are not transmitted to us. You can delete them at any time via your browser settings.

Unlocking the evaluation. If you unlock the full evaluation, we transmit your email address, your organisation, your role (if provided), your answer values and the result to a database service (Supabase). The purpose is to offer you an interpretation of your result. The legal basis is your consent pursuant to Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, which you give by ticking the box in the form and which you can withdraw at any time with effect for the future. The data are processed jointly with Schubart & Dulgeridis GmbH (https://www.schubart-dulgeridis.de/). We do not send a newsletter.

Team profile. If you create a team profile, only the eight answer values are stored, without name or email address. Only the average is visible, and only once there are at least five responses. The data are automatically deleted after 90 days.

Invitation to compare. The invitation link contains your eight answer values but no name and no email address. You decide for yourself whom to invite.

When a connection to the database service is established, technically necessary connection data (for example, the IP address) are transmitted to its servers.

7. External links

This website links to external sites, for example the partners' LinkedIn profiles or the parent company's website. No third-party plugins or embedded content are used; data are only transmitted when you click a link. The operators of the linked sites are responsible for their content.

8. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21). You can withdraw any consent you have given at any time with effect for the future. To exercise your rights, please contact us at the contact address given above.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. The authority responsible for us is the Bavarian State Office for Data Protection Supervision (Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht, BayLDA), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach.

9. Changes

We update this policy when technology or the legal situation changes. The version published here at any given time applies.
