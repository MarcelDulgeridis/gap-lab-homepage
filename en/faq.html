Company.

What does THE GAP Lab do?

It designs, develops, delivers and markets people development programmes, such as sales and negotiation training, leadership development and financial literacy. Its focus areas are Strategy, Sales & Innovation, Leadership & Organisation and Finance & Transformation. The aim: strategy that reaches everyday work.

Who is behind THE GAP Lab?

The Managing Director is Prof. Dr. Constantin Schubart; the partners are Dr. Oliver Meschkat and Prof. Dr. Marcel Dulgeridis. Constantin Schubart and Marcel Dulgeridis are Professors of Business Administration at IU International University of Applied Sciences; Oliver Meschkat specialises in marketing and sales management, people development, and innovation and change management. Profiles: About us.

What is the difference between THE GAP Lab and Schubart & Dulgeridis GmbH?

Schubart & Dulgeridis GmbH offers research-based consulting and diagnostics (THE GAP, Pulse Check, upskilling, coaching, recruiting). THE GAP Lab develops and delivers people development programmes. The two companies are affiliated, with THE GAP Lab as the subsidiary: schubart-dulgeridis.de.

Who are the services intended for?

For senior management, HR managers and leaders who plan, deliver or purchase development in a targeted way. Chambers, training providers and academies also use the InnoLab formats.

Where is THE GAP Lab based?

In Altdorf bei Nürnberg (Hessenstraße 28, 90518). Full company details are in the Legal notice.

What is meant by the “gap”?

The gap between what is decided and what reaches everyday work. This is where strategy, leadership and impact get lost. THE GAP Lab's programmes address exactly this point.

Services.

What does THE GAP Lab offer?

People development programmes in four stages: Concept, Development, Delivery and Marketing. In terms of content, the partners work in three focus areas.

Do I have to commission all four stages?

No. One stage, several stages or the entire chain: we clarify this in the free initial consultation based on your situation.

What outcomes will I receive?

Depending on the stage: a concept with goals and success criteria, materials and exercises, a delivered programme with evaluation, or a clear programme description. We set out the exact scope in the proposal. Overview: Services.

Do you also offer diagnostics?

Diagnostics such as THE GAP and the Pulse Check are offered by Schubart & Dulgeridis GmbH: schubart-dulgeridis.de/the-gap. THE GAP Lab focuses on people development programmes.

What do the key questions of the four stages mean?

Concept: What should the programme achieve, and for whom? Development: How does the concept become practice for participants? Delivery: In which format is the programme most effective? Marketing: How are the benefits and the target group made clear?

Focus areas.

What does Strategy, Sales & Innovation cover?

Marketing and sales management, strategic management, negotiation, rhetoric, facilitation, conflict management, team building, and innovation and change management. The contact person is Dr. Oliver Meschkat. More on this focus area.

What is negotiation management?

The purposeful preparation, management and evaluation of negotiations. The goal is outcomes that protect your own interests and are viable enough for the other side to be implemented. More on this focus area.

What do BATNA and ZOPA mean?

BATNA is the best alternative if no agreement is reached (Best Alternative to a Negotiated Agreement); ZOPA is the range within which both sides could agree (Zone of Possible Agreement). The Glossary explains both terms.

What is the difference between negotiation and rhetoric?

Negotiation concerns strategy and preparation: goals, alternatives, room for manoeuvre and the course of the conversation. Rhetoric concerns impact: structuring arguments, choice of words and presence. The two complement each other.

What does Leadership & Organisation mean?

Leadership is understood not as a trait of individuals but as the interplay of strategy, culture, roles and decision-making paths. The goal is clear expectations and decisions that take hold in everyday work. More on this focus area.

Do I need a diagnostic assessment first?

Not necessarily. If you want to measure the gap between leadership aspirations and everyday work first, you will find THE GAP at Schubart & Dulgeridis GmbH. However, programmes can also be designed without prior diagnostics.

What does Finance & Transformation cover?

Financial literacy (accounting, key figures, economic viability) combined with implementing change, so that decisions are strategically sound and economically robust. More on this focus area.

Do managers need prior business knowledge?

No. Programmes can be adapted to the target group's prior knowledge. The goal is understanding, not bookkeeping.

What is the difference between profit and cash flow?

Profit is the result of the income statement for a period; cash flow is the balance of actual payments. The two can differ considerably, for example when invoices are paid later.

InnoLab.

What is InnoLab?

A toolkit of five workshop formats for innovation, change and learning, held on site at your premises. Each format addresses a typical task and ends with an outcome you can continue to use. Go to the InnoLab page.

What are the five formats?

InnoLab Sprint (innovation week, 5 days), InnoLab Design (learning space consultancy, 3 days), Learning Design 4.0 (train the trainer, 3 days), Change Leadership (leadership training, 2 + 1 days) and Innovation Scorecard (strategy workshop, 2 days).

Which InnoLab format is right for us?

The Sprint suits prototyping on a real problem, Design suits learning spaces, Learning Design 4.0 suits instructors and trainers, Change Leadership suits managers and change agents, and the Scorecard suits managing innovation with KPIs. We clarify this together in the free introductory session.

Where does InnoLab take place, and how long does it last?

All formats take place on site at your premises and last between two and five days. You provide the room and catering; concept, organisation and travel time are included in the package price.

What are the prerequisites?

Depending on the format: a flexible room, a real problem and decision-makers on pitch day (Sprint), a real project (Change Leadership) or one of your own seminars to be revised (Learning Design 4.0). We clarify this in the introductory session.

How are prices, payment and cancellation handled?

All prices and terms are listed on the InnoLab page in the Prices section.

Are the InnoLab formats eligible for funding?

Funding through INQA-Coaching for small and medium-sized enterprises is possible on request. We check whether your project is eligible in a preliminary meeting.

Who delivers InnoLab?

Dr. Oliver Meschkat, Partner for Innovation and Change. He wrote his doctorate on the performance of German innovation labs and has worked as a trainer and consultant since 2010. The formats are provided by Schubart & Dulgeridis GmbH, the parent company of THE GAP Lab.

AI Readiness Check.

What is the AI Readiness Check?

A free check that asks about eight signals of your organisation's AI maturity in three minutes, compares the views of management and employees, and shows the AI gap and the most important area for action. Go to the Check.

How long does the Check take, and what does it cost?

It takes about three minutes, is free of charge and requires no login. The result appears immediately.

What happens to my answers in the Check?

Your answers stay in your browser. Only if you unlock the full evaluation do we store your email address, organisation and result in order to offer you an interpretation. A team view stores only the eight answer values, without name or email, in the EU and deletes them after 90 days. Details: Privacy policy.

How reliable is the result?

It is an honest self-assessment from a single perspective and therefore a directional signal, not a certification. How big the gap really is only becomes clear through a real survey of leadership and employees.

Working together.

How do we start working together?

It starts with a free initial consultation (20 to 30 minutes, Microsoft Teams) on the reason, goal and target group. You then receive a proposal with a clearly defined scope of services. Appointment: Contact.

What does working together cost?

How does a programme work?

In four steps: initial consultation (reason, goal, target group), concept (goals, content, format, success criteria), implementation (development and delivery) and evaluation (review impact, agree next steps).
